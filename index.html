Our Story

A Passion for Authentic Cuisine

Founded in 2018 by Chef Antoine Marchand, Saveur was born from a simple belief — that great food has the power to bring people together. Inspired by his grandmother's kitchen in Lyon, Chef Antoine brings French culinary tradition to every plate.

We source only the finest seasonal ingredients from local farmers and artisans, crafting dishes that celebrate both heritage and innovation. Every detail — from the plating to the ambiance — is designed to create an unforgettable dining experience.
